Move with the mouse, drag on touch screens, or use ↑ / ↓. Click with the mouse or press Space to shoot; repeat to reload. On touch screens, drag only moves the archer; use Shoot/Reload to fire. Full Screen expands the game to the available screen. Press P or Escape to pause. Arrows can hit multiple balloons. The last hit at the time limit wins. Avoid yellow balloons in level 2: they cost 10 points. Valid targets return one arrow; every 10 hits in a level award 100 points and a magic feather. Unused arrows and feathers carry to the next level. In level 3, pop the bubbles to free the butterflies. Progress is saved between levels. After defeat or reopening the game, use Continue to retry from the latest checkpoint. In level 4, shoot or dodge the swamp slimes. Magic feathers protect against contact; without one, a hit ends the game. In level 5, aim at the gold center of the moving target; outer rings absorb missed shots. In level 6, shoot or dodge the incoming fireballs; feathers protect against contact. In level 7, shoot every vulture and protect the white dove. Do not shoot the messenger or let enemies pass.
